Vision

The vision is simple — a welcoming place where the community can encounter Jesus through genuine love and everyday connection. A church that feels like a community center. A space that brings together the warmth of a café, the comfort of a living room, and the openness of a gathering place.

We believe we should be one body in Christ — using our individual gifts and talents to serve our amazing Piedmont community. As followers of Christ, we have the answer the world is seeking, and an opportunity to build something beautiful together.

"Do not despise these small beginnings." — Zechariah 4:10
What you'll find

A Free Place
for Everyone

Come in, take a breath, share coffee, bring the kids, meet a friend, or simply spend time in a safe and caring place. No cost. No catch. All welcome.

Coffee & Connection
Free coffee, tea, juice, and snacks with room to sit, talk, and build real friendships.
Faith & Encouragement
Worship, prayer, Bible studies, and a short daily message centered on Jesus.
Play & Belonging
Indoor and outdoor play, games, books, and space for every generation.
Service & Community
A community garden and practical ways to care for neighbors together.

The Jesus Barn will serve as a lighthouse in Piedmont — helping people find hope, safety, community, and the arms of Jesus.
